Help and support

Contact us

Found a bug, lost progress, or a purchase didn't arrive? Email ethan.ektan@gmail.com with your iPhone model and what happened. We usually reply within two working days.

A purchase didn't arrive

Close the game completely and open it again: unfinished purchases are delivered at launch. For Remove Ads on a new iPhone, open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases.

How do I get more energy?

Energy refills by one every 3 minutes, and finishing a chapter gives a bonus. You can also watch a short optional video (a few times a day) or refill with gems.

Can I change a choice I made?

Yes. Open Story so far › Story Map. Paths you have already taken can be switched back to for free; a new path can be unlocked with gems.

Where are hearts used?

Hearts show how close Owen, Julian and Conrad feel to Ivy. Reaching certain hearts unlocks hidden scenes and endings. Tap a character in Story so far to see their love track.

Will my progress be saved?

Progress is saved on your iPhone automatically and included in your iCloud backup. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Turning off reminders or sound

Tap the ⚙️ button at the top of the game to turn sound effects, music and reminders on or off.
