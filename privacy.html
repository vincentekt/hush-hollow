Hush Hollow

Privacy Policy · last updated 1 November 2026

Hush Hollow is a story and merge puzzle game for iPhone. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. In short: we don't ask for your name, email or any account, and your story progress stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your progress (chapter, choices, coins, gems and settings) is saved on your iPhone. A copy is kept in the app's own storage so it survives updates, and it is included in your device's iCloud or computer backup if you use one. We do not have a server that stores your progress.

Purchases

In-app purchases (gems, the starter pack and Remove Ads) are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation from Apple that a purchase was made so it can give you what you bought.

Advertising

Hush Hollow shows optional reward videos and an occasional ad between chapters, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when the app asks), your approximate location from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. The Remove Ads purchase turns off the ads between chapters. Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which chapters players enjoy and where they get stuck, the game may send anonymous events such as "chapter 3 started" or "rescue won" to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. These events carry a random identifier created on your device, not your name, email, advertising identifier or contact details, and they can't be used to identify you. See TelemetryDeck's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your iPhone itself (for example, when your energy is full). They are created on the device and no information is sent to us to do this. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in iPhone Settings.

Children

Hush Hollow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Turn off tracking for ads in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Turn off reminders in the game's Settings.
  • Delete all game data by deleting the app.
